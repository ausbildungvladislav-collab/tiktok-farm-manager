Authentication and authorization use TikTok's official OAuth flow. Authorization data is used to perform actions authorized by the user and is not intentionally shared with unrelated third parties.

Video files selected by the user are processed to carry out the requested publishing workflow.

The application does not sell personal information. Information is shared with TikTok only as necessary to provide the requested integration.

5. Security

Reasonable technical measures are used to protect application and authorization data.

6. Retention and deletion

Data is retained only as reasonably necessary to provide the requested functionality or meet legal obligations. Users may request deletion through the contact method provided by the application owner.
